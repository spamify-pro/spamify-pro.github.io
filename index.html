    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    Does it send my data to any external service?

    No. The engine runs entirely on your server. SMTP checks talk directly to the recipient's own mail server (you can switch this off), CAPTCHA is opt-in with your own keys, and the geo database is stored locally. Nothing about your visitors is sent to us.

    Will it block real customers by mistake?

    Every layer is tunable. Set an allowlist for trusted domains, emails and IPs, and choose per-signal whether to block or just flag for the Review Queue. Flag Threshold only flags; only Block Threshold rejects.

    What if spam already got into my site?

    Run the Spam Scan. It scores existing users and comments in safe batches, shows the evidence, and never offers admins, editors or your own account for deletion.

    Do I need a paid CAPTCHA service?

    No. CAPTCHA is optional and uses your own free Cloudflare Turnstile or Google reCAPTCHA v3 keys. The engine already stops most spam without it.

    What's the difference between Free and Pro?

    Free covers email validation, bot and behaviour checks, rate limiting, the Review Queue, Timeline, digests and hide-login. Pro adds disposable-domain and IP threat intel, VPN/Tor detection, content analysis, the firewall, the geo report, deliverability tools and every integration.

    Is it GDPR-friendly?

    Yes — optional IP anonymisation, automatic log purging on a schedule (or on demand), and zero third-party data sharing by design.

    Is it available in my language?

    Admin screens and visitor-facing messages are translated into German, Spanish and French, and every string is translation-ready for more.
