WPForms, Jetpack, and Elementor: One Engine for Every Form Builder

You shouldn’t need a different anti-spam plugin for each form builder. Spamify protects WPForms, Jetpack Forms, and Elementor Forms with the same engine and the same settings.

SpamifyUpdated Jul 27, 20265 min read

Most sites end up running more than one form builder — WPForms for lead capture, Jetpack’s block for a quick contact form, Elementor’s widget inside a landing page. Protecting each with a different tool means different settings, different logs, and gaps between them. Spamify’s answer is one engine behind all of them.

Same protection, every builder

Enable the integrations you use in the setup wizard and each builder’s submissions flow through the identical pipeline:

  • WPForms — every email field gets syntax and optional mailbox validation; the whole submission is scored and honeypot/timing checked.
  • Jetpack Forms — the block-editor contact form is covered without any block configuration.
  • Elementor Forms — the form widget is protected wherever you drop it, including inside templates and popups.

One set of settings to reason about

Because it’s a single engine, your thresholds, rate limits, allowlist, and retention settings apply consistently across builders. Tune once, and every form benefits — no per-plugin re-learning.

One dashboard for everything

Blocked and flagged submissions from all builders land in the same dashboard. That unified view matters: it’s how you spot that an attack is hitting your Elementor landing page while your WPForms contact form is quiet, and respond in one place instead of three.

Adding builders later

Install a new form plugin down the road? Enable its Spamify integration and it inherits your existing configuration immediately. Your spam defense scales with your site instead of fragmenting across it.
